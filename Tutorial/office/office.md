Intro

Are you still stuck with Microsoft Office 2010 and don't know how to update to the newest version? Don't worry! As long as you have a Microsoft account with an Office license, you will be able to update your Office to the newest version and even add Visio to your computer. In this tutorial, I'll guide you step by step on how to update your Office to the latest version!



Step 1: Uninstall Your Old Office Application

First, back up any Office data you have to a new directory. Once you've done that, press the Windows key, search for Control Panel, and click on Programs, then Programs and Features. Find all the apps that start with Microsoft Office (and Microsoft Visio, if you had it before). Left-click the Office/Visio app and click the Uninstall button below the Uninstall or change a program headline. Wait a couple of minutes for the process to complete.



Step 2: Download the Office Deployment Tool

After completing the first step, open the browser of your choice, search for Office Deployment Tool, and make sure the link is from Microsoft. Click the first result that pops up. You should see an Office image headline; below it, choose your language and hit Download.



Once it finishes downloading, run the .exe file, agree to the license terms, and create a new folder named "office" during the setup process. Select the folder you just made and click OK. Finally, cut and paste the office folder (with the extracted files inside) directly into your C: drive (your local disk/Windows disk).



Step 3: Configure the Office Customization Tool

Head back to your browser, search for Office Customization Tool, and click the first result (make sure the URL is config.office.com).



Architecture: Under the Office Customization Tool headline, select your system architecture (32-bit or 64-bit). If you don't know your architecture, press the Windows key, search for About, and check your System type under Device specifications.



Products: Scroll down to the Products section. You will see four options: Office, Visio, Project, and Additional products. Select the products you need. For this tutorial, we will select Office LTSC Standard 2024 and Visio LTSC Standard 2024.



Apps: Scroll down to the Apps section and toggle off any apps you don't need (for example, Publisher, Outlook, OneDrive, or OneNote) to save storage space. Click Next.



Language: Select your primary language and click Next.



Installation \& Upgrade Options: Skip the Installation section. In the Update and upgrade options section, uncheck Uninstall any MSI versions (since we already uninstalled the old version for a clean install).



Export: Click Next through the remaining sections (Licensing, General, and Application preferences). On the right side, click the top Export button. Select Keep Current Settings, accept the license agreement, and name your file Config to keep things simple.



Move File: Cut the newly downloaded Config.xml file and paste it into the office folder on your C: drive. Delete the default configuration files generated by the Deployment Tool (be careful not to delete setup.exe), then paste your Config.xml file there.



Step 4: Installation

Open PowerShell or Command Prompt as an administrator. Copy the path of the office folder on your C: drive where your config file is located.



In PowerShell/CMD, type:



Bash

cd C:\\office

(Replace C:\\office with your actual folder path if different) and press Enter.



Next, type:



Bash

setup /configure Config.xml

Press Enter. A Microsoft setup window will pop up—and voilà! You are now installing the new, updated Office apps. It's that easy!
