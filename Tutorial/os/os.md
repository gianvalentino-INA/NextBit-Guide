IntroIs your PC or laptop starting to feel sluggish during everyday use? Don't worry! Your device will run like brand new after you follow this tutorial. All you need is a USB drive (at least 8 GB), the right operating system, and a few minutes to follow these steps.Step 1: Preparing Your USB DriveFirst, head over to ventoy.net to download the Ventoy software. Ventoy makes your USB drive bootable and allows you to store multiple operating system ISOs (such as Windows and various Linux distributions) on a single drive.Once the download is complete, extract the Ventoy folder and open it.Run the Ventoy2Disk.exe file.Select your plugged-in USB drive from the drop-down list and click Install.⚠️ CAUTION:Installing Ventoy will completely reformat your USB drive and erase all existing data on it. Make sure to back up any important files to another storage device before proceeding.Accept the prompts to begin the formatting process.Once the installation is complete, click Update to ensure Ventoy is running the latest version.Close the Ventoy application and move on to the next step.Step 2: Choosing and Downloading the OS Image (ISO)In this step, you need to make an important decision: Windows or Linux?Windows vs. Linux ComparisonFeatureWindowsLinuxBest ForGeneral compatibility, familiarity, & hassle-free gamingOlder/low-end devices, full control, & lightweight performanceUser ExperiencePlug-and-play, easy out-of-the-box experienceRequires a learning curve and occasional tinkeringHardware SupportWorks automatically with almost all hardwareRequires checking component compatibility (e.g., Wi-Fi cards)ℹ️ NOTE:For this tutorial, we will use Windows 11 as our primary example.How to Download the Windows ISO:Open your web browser, search for Windows 11 ISO, and click the official microsoft.com link.Scroll down to the "Download Windows 11 Disk Image (ISO) for x64 devices" section.Under Select Download, choose Windows 11 (multi-edition ISO for x64 devices) and click Download.Once downloaded, cut and paste the ISO file directly into your Ventoy USB drive.ℹ️ NOTE:Copy the ISO directly to the root of the Ventoy USB drive—do not hide it inside deep subfolders.Step 3: The Installation ProcessShut down your PC or laptop.Turn the device back on while repeatedly pressing your BIOS key.ℹ️ NOTE:The BIOS key varies depending on your device manufacturer, but common keys include F2, Delete, F10, Esc, or F12.Once inside the BIOS menu, locate the Boot tab (if you don't see it, check under Advanced Settings).Change Boot Option 1 to your Ventoy USB drive.Save your settings and reboot your system.Once the Ventoy menu loads, use the arrow keys to select your desired ISO file (e.g., Windows 11 or Linux) and press Enter.Windows Installation StepsChoose your language and keyboard layout (default is US).Click Install Windows 11.Accept the license agreement.⚠️ CAUTION:Custom/Clean installation will delete your files, applications, and settings on the target drive partition. Ensure your important data is backed up.On the drive partitioning screen, select the target drive partition, delete older partitions if necessary, click Next, and let the installation complete.Complete the initial setup screen (account creation, regional settings, etc.).Linux Installation Steps (Example: Fedora)Select the Fedora ISO from the Ventoy menu and boot into the environment.Wait for the initial system commands to run until you enter the Live Boot desktop.ℹ️ NOTE:Use the Live Desktop environment to test your hardware components—especially Wi-Fi cards, touchpads, and webcams—to confirm everything works out-of-the-box before installing.Double-click the Install to Hard Drive app on the desktop.Follow the step-by-step installer to configure your target drive, username, and system options.Step 4: Windows ActivationIf you do not have a digital license linked to your Microsoft account, you can remove the activation watermark using the open-source MAS tool.Open PowerShell on your newly installed Windows system.Run the following command:PowerShellirm https://get.activated.win | iex
